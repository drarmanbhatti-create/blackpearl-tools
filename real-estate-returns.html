        Which holding structure fits your case?

        This widget computes a model. Whether CFC taxation, § 23 EStG, exit taxation or Free Zone status apply in your specific case is decided by details no calculator knows. We review your constellation in 30 minutes.

        Model assumptions and legal basis

        Acquisition costs: DLD transfer 4 % plus AED 580 admin, trustee AED 4,200 plus 5 % VAT, title deed AED 500, NOC AED 2,000, agent 2 % plus VAT, valuation AED 3,000, mortgage registration 0.25 % plus AED 290, bank fee 1 % (the last three only where debt-financed).

        Operations: vacancy 6 % of PGI, service charge 18 AED/sqft, maintenance and property management 5 % of EGI each, insurance AED 3,000; rent and cost growth 3 % p.a. each.

        Financing: annuity loan, 25 years, monthly payment. Debt sizing as a double test: loan = MIN(LTV limit; present value of the debt service sustainable at the minimum DSCR). Regulatory LTV cap 60 %.

        UAE: corporate tax 9 % above AED 375,000 (Federal Decree-Law No. 47/2022). Natural persons are not taxable on privately held real estate (Cabinet Decision No. 49/2023). Residential property regularly yields no Qualifying Income for a Free Zone person (Cabinet Decision No. 100/2023), hence 9 % in the QFZP case as well. Nexus of non-resident juridical persons: Cabinet Decision No. 56/2023. Interest limitation: 30 % EBITDA, safe harbour AED 12m.

        Germany: the Germany–UAE double taxation agreement (2010) expired on 31 December 2021; no successor agreement exists. Exemption therefore does not apply; what remains is credit relief under § 34c EStG and § 26 KStG respectively. Building depreciation 2 % (§ 7(4) EStG) on 80 % of the purchase price. Marginal rate 45 % plus 5.5 % solidarity surcharge = 47.475 %; corporate income tax incl. solidarity surcharge 15.825 %, trade tax reduced under § 9 no. 3 GewStG.

        CFC rules: for the Free Zone company §§ 7 et seq. AStG apply, because control, passive income and low taxation (9 % < 15 %, § 8(5) AStG) are present and the substance test of § 8(2) AStG is not available for third countries; the UAE tax is credited under § 12 AStG. On exit from private assets, § 23 EStG applies within the ten-year period.

        All figures are a non-binding model calculation based on the parameters entered and do not replace tax or legal advice in an individual case. EUR/AED conversion at 4.2769. Legal position: 2026.
