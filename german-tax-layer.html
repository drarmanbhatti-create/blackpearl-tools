    E. § 2 AStG — extended limited tax liability

    Applies only to non-foreign income of a German national who was subject to unlimited tax liability for at least five of the last ten years and moves to a low-tax country (§ 2(1), (2) AStG). The UAE levies no income tax, so this criterion is met. The decisive point: only non-foreign income within the meaning of § 34d EStG is captured. Dubai rental income is foreign income under § 34d no. 7 EStG and therefore falls outside the scope; § 2 AStG reaches the German assets left behind, not the new Emirati ones. Exemption limit EUR 16,500 (§ 2(1) sentence 3 AStG), applicable for ten years after unlimited tax liability ends.
