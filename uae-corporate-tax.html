9% UAE Corporate Tax is only half the calculation. Without a double taxation agreement, the German side determines what remains of the saving.

Legal basis. Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 (Corporate Tax Law) together with Cabinet Decisions 49/2023, 55/2023, 56/2023 and 100/2023. Natural persons are not subject to corporate tax on privately held real estate outside a licensing requirement (Art. 11(6) CT Law in conjunction with Cabinet Decision 49/2023). A Free Zone company regularly does not derive Qualifying Income from residential real estate (Cabinet Decision 100/2023) and therefore effectively pays the same rate as a Mainland company. Nexus of a non-resident juridical person: Cabinet Decision 56/2023. Interest limitation: 30% of tax EBITDA, safe harbour AED 12m net interest expense. Small Business Relief: Ministerial Decision No. 131 of 2026 of 29 July 2026, revenue threshold AED 3m, extended to tax periods ending on or before 31 December 2029.

Methodology. NOI as in the retail module, multiplied by the portfolio factor. Year 1 interest expense from the annuity calculation. Building depreciation under UAE accounting on a straight-line basis over the useful life set above, independent of German depreciation. Tax base = portfolio NOI less interest expense less depreciation, reduced by the AED 375,000 threshold (not for natural persons, who are not liable in principle). All figures are a non-binding model calculation and do not replace tax advice in an individual case.
