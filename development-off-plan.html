Development and Off-Plan: Does the Construction Risk Pay Off?

A project only carries its completion risk if the return on its own development cost lies clearly above the return the market will later grant a buyer. The model builds up cost year by year, capitalises construction interest, maps the lease-up phase and splits the surplus between investors and sponsor.

The model calculates before tax and at project level. How you participate — directly, through a UAE company or a German holding — determines what reaches you after German taxation.

Methodology. Land in year 0, construction over three years following the cost curve set above. The construction loan is drawn pro rata on each cost instalment; construction interest is capitalised on a mid-year convention and increases the loan balance. After completion a bullet structure applies: interest paid currently, principal repaid at exit. Exit proceeds = the following year's net rent divided by the exit cap rate. Yield on cost = stabilised net rent (third year after completion) divided by total development cost including construction interest.

Profit split. Simplified European waterfall: each investor cash flow is compounded forward to exit at the preferred return. Only a positive surplus above that is shared with the sponsor in the ratio set above, payable at exit.

Reference values. A development spread below 150 basis points and a profit on cost below 15 % are regarded institutionally as not commensurate with the risk. All figures are a non-binding model calculation and do not replace advice in an individual case. Conversion at 4.2769 AED per EUR.
