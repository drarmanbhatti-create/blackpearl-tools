BLACK PEARL · Analytical Tools

Analytical tools

Four self-contained calculation modules prepared for embedding into the BLACK PEARL website. Each module runs standalone, without a build step and without external scripts.

Working state — not published. This page exists so the modules have a stable address for embedding. Content, legal positions and terminology have not yet been reviewed by the owner. Nothing here is approved for public use.

/tools/uae-corporate-tax

UAE Corporate Tax

Four holding structures under one tax regime, scaled from a single property to portfolio size. Includes the interest deduction limitation and Small Business Relief.

/tools/german-tax-layer

German Tax Layer

Ongoing taxation of four structures without a double taxation agreement, plus § 23 EStG, § 6 AStG exit taxation, the CFC test cascade and § 2a EStG.

/tools/real-estate-returns

Real Estate Returns

NOI, cash-on-cash, DSCR and after-tax return, with a year-one tax bridge between the UAE and German levels and a five-year cash flow.

/tools/development-off-plan

Development & Off-Plan

Development spread against the exit cap rate, cost and capital build-up, profit on cost, and the split between investors and sponsor.

Embedding. Each module reports its height to the embedding window via postMessage as { type:'bp-tool-height', tool:<name>, height:<px> }, and reports a booking intent as { type:'bp-tool-cta', tool:<name>, action:'book-consultation' }. The booking flow itself is not wired up; the button is inert until a provider is chosen.

Review required before publication. Legal positions, statutory citations, the English rendering of German tax terminology and all model assumptions are carried over from the source modules and have not been verified.
